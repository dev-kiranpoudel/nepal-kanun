# Privacy Policy: नेपालको कानून

Applies to the Android app **नेपालको कानून** (`com.nepalkokanun.app`). Last updated: 16 October 2026.

## What the app does

The app is a library of Nepal's acts, regulations, ordinances and policies. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not ask for your name, phone number, email or location.

## What stays on your phone

- Laws you pin, laws you opened recently and the page you stopped on.
- PDFs you open, saved so you can read them offline. You can remove them in More.
- Your language and theme choices.

This information is not sent to us.

## What the app downloads

- The law list, once a day, from `raw.githubusercontent.com` (GitHub).
- A law's PDF when you open it, from the Nepal Law Commission's servers.

Those services can see your IP address and device details, as any website would. We do not control their logs.

## Third-party services

The app uses Google Firebase, which can collect technical and usage information such as app version, device model, Android version, crash and performance data, and an anonymous app instance ID. Google processes it under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app shows no ads.

## Children

The app is not aimed at children and knowingly collects no personal data from anyone.

## Disclaimer

This app is not affiliated with the Government of Nepal. It is for information only and is not legal advice. The Nepal Gazette is the authoritative text. English titles are unofficial translations.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will appear on this page. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/dev-kiranpoudel/nepal-kanun/issues
